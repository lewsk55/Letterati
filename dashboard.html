Win rate and where runs fail

Win rate by ascension

Finished runs only (won, lost, or ended). Wins over finished runs.

Lever: the target multiplier and rule each rung adds (runTargetMult, the A-rung rules).

Win rate by character

Same runs, split by who was at the press.

Lever: character perks and the Editor's discard cut.

Where lost runs end

Round in which each lost run ran out of hearts. Ended (quit) runs are counted below, not here.

Lever: the target curve for the round where losses pile up (targetFor, GOAL_SCALE), and that round's words and discards.

Round by round

Every round played in the filtered runs. A clean clear reaches the target without an overtime word. An overtime word costs a heart unless Deadline Extension or Midnight Press covers it.

Score / target is the median for cleared rounds. Discards count the ones actually spent (fixed in 0.51a; older builds could undercount). House discards are the free ones given for a dead hand.

Lever: a low clean-clear rate or a high overtime rate points at that round's target. A wide score / target means the round is easy.

Bosses and challenges

Bosses

Final-proof bosses faced in the filtered runs. Words to clear is the median for beaten bosses.

Lever: The Deadline raises the target 10% after every word that falls short. The Ghost's quota comes from the predictor's own run on the seed.

Challenges

Optional and required challenge forks. Cleared is a share of challenges taken.

Lever: a clear rate near 100% means a challenge is a free reward. Harder rules or smaller rewards add risk.

Economy and shop

Coins in and out by round

Average coins paid at each round's clear, and average coins spent in the shop that follows it.

Earned at clearSpent in next shop

Lever: the purse (RUN_PAY_SCALE, word cash, side jobs) against shop prices. Spend tracking well under income means coins pile up unused.

Where the coins go

Coins spent per finished run, by kind of purchase.

Lever: gild prices (gildCostFor) and fusion cost (fusionCost, 5 to 20).

Opening perk draft

Free pick of 1 from 3 before each run. Pick rate is picks over times offered.

Lever: the draft pool. A perk nobody picks is dead weight in it.

Shop items

Every shelf appearance, rerolled shelves included (0.51a and later; older builds logged the opening shelf only). Undone purchases are excluded. Items grouped by family; at least 3 appearances.

Lever: price and shelf weight. A buy rate near zero at a fair price means the item doesn't earn its slot.
